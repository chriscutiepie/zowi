Topic

Playing volleyball is more than just a fun game, it’s a way for students to stay active and healthy. The sport helps improve strength, coordination, and fitness while also boosting focus, teamwork, and mood. By learning from students who play volleyball, this study shows how the sport supports both physical and mental well-being. Encouraging volleyball in schools can help students become healthier, happier, and more active every day.
